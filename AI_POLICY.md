# AI Usage Policy

This fork permits AI-assisted contributions, including code, tests,
documentation, issues, and pull requests.

Contributors are responsible for reviewing and validating all submitted
work, checking its security and licensing, preserving attribution for
ported code, and disclosing material AI assistance. Do not submit secrets,
unlicensed third-party content, or generated media without the rights and
provenance needed to distribute it.

AI assistance does not replace human review. Keep contributions focused,
tested, and useful to maintainers and users.
